The design of this keyboard needed to meet the following criteria:

1. A split keyboard with a low profile for easy carriage
2. Bluetooth connectivity so that I can connect it to various devices (Maximum 2-3)
3. Try a new physical layout (I went with Ortholinear with staggered columns)
4. Make it hotswappable and as open-source as possible

With these rulings in mind, after doing some digging into pre-existing designs and models, I came across the "Fortitude60" keyboard as an inspiration for my layout.
